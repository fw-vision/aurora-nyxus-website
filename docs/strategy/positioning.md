# Aurora Nyxus - Positioning (public-safe)

The subset of strategy that informs the website. Sanitised for a public repo:
no ownership, no interim legal vehicle, no partner-private detail.

## Thesis

A premium Canadian AI compute builder that earns trust through excellent
workstation builds, then converts that trust into sovereign on-prem
deployments. Two doors, one credibility flywheel.

```
Build excellent workstations -> earn references + revenue
  -> win sovereign on-prem deployments
  -> DAI Compute partnership + larger installs
  -> back to more builds
```

## Two doors

- **Door 1 - Premium AI workstations.** Revenue + credibility engine. Direct competitor to Alpha PC. Quote-based custom builds. Segments: research labs, quant & finance, studios.
- **Door 2 - Sovereign on-prem deployment.** Strategic, defensible. Data-residency-sensitive work. Segments: government / dual-use, Indigenous data sovereignty, regulated enterprise. Sovereignty + finance via DAI Compute partnership.

## Differentiation

1. Sovereignty-first, not an afterthought.
2. Oxide-class brand in a gamer-storefront market.
3. Dogfood credibility (we run our own workloads; the break-even is real).
4. Scrappy meshed capacity (old + new hardware) as a capability.
5. Cheap Ontario power + Canadian custody.

## Horizons

1. Prove the craft (RTX 5090 node + two-node mesh; dogfood).
2. First revenue + references (site live, first paid builds).
3. Sovereign deployments (gov / Indigenous / regulated enterprise).
4. Rack-scale, competing with Oxide-class integrated platforms.

## Segment landing strategy

Each buyer persona gets a dedicated landing page speaking its language and
stakes, rather than one generic page. Door 1: labs (VRAM, stability, scale),
quant (private, low-latency, discreet), studios (throughput, render-stable).
Door 2: government (CLOUD-Act-free, auditable), Indigenous data (physical
custody, OCAP), enterprise (air-gapped, compliance-ready).
